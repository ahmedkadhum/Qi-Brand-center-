Section 06 — Our Graphic Device

The extended circle.

A half circle taken from the Qi icon, then stretched into an elongated cylinder. Used to house type and the Qi icon when standing against an image background — anywhere copy needs to read clearly without dropping the photo.

51 — Constructing graphic devices

Start with the icon. Crop. Extend.

  1. Start — Begin with the Qi icon at full size.
  2. Crop — Crop along the vertical centre line of the icon, keeping the right semicircle.
  3. Extend — Stretch that cropped edge horizontally to create an elongated cylinder.

The shape is useful when housing type and the Qi icon to stand out against an image background.

52 — Graphic devices usage

Houses copy on busy backgrounds.

Extending the half circle yields a larger device perfect for housing additional information and setting it apart from the background image. Place it bottom-half (Example 1) or upper-half (Example 2) of the layout — never in the middle.

53 — Graphic devices misuse

Four things to avoid.

01 · Too close to the edge

Placing the half-circle device too close to the edge creates an imbalance of space.

02 · Full circle

The only full circle in the system is the Qi icon. Don't draw a full-circle device.

03 · Vertical orientation

Various uses of the device may be explored in future. For now, keeping things simple is always key — don't rotate it vertical.

04 · Middle of layout

Never place the device in the middle of the layout. It belongs to the top or bottom half.
