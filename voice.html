Foundation / Voice
Section 08

Voice & tone.

Qi sounds the way it looks: clear, confident, and warm. Three principles guide every line we write — in apps, ads, customer service, and the quiet back-of-card copy nobody is supposed to notice.

"Easy payments will make you stress less."

On this page

  1. Three principles
  2. Do & don't
  3. Using the Qi name
  4. In context
01

Three principles.

01

Clear.

Short words. Active sentences. If a customer has to re-read a line, it isn't done.

02

Confident.

We know what we're doing — but we don't show off. Specific beats vague every time.

03

Warm.

Talk like a friend who happens to know banking. Never patronising, never cold.

02

Do & don't.

✓ Do
  • "Pay with one tap. No card needed."
  • "Your salary just landed."
  • "Get cash from any Qi ATM, free."
  • Use everyday words: send, get, save, easy.
✕ Don't
  • "Leverage seamless payment infrastructures."
  • "Salary credited to designated account."
  • "Withdrawals from member ATMs incur zero charges."
  • Don't use jargon, acronyms, or legal-speak in marketing.
03

Using the Qi name.

Always write the brand as Qi — capital Q, lowercase i — never QI, qi, or Qi. with a trailing period unless the sentence requires it. In sentence form, treat it as a proper noun.

Right

Open a Qi account in two minutes.

Wrong

Open a QI account in two minutes.

04
