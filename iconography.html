Section 04 — Our Iconography

Built on a 10×10 grid.

Every Qi icon is constructed on a 10×10 modular grid of 15×15mm cells, with two line weights — 10mm thick and 7.5mm thin — and 5mm spaces. Most icons live in a 3D space, rendered for a tangible, color-on-color look. There are 14 named icons in the system today.

40 — Iconography construction

10 × 10 modular grid.

All iconography is built on a 10×10 modular grid composed of 15×15mm cells. Internal circles act as a visual reference to the Qi icon. Two diagonal lines drawn from the corners give additional alignment points.

Cell
15 × 15 mm

The base unit of the icon grid.

Thick line
10 mm

Used for primary outlines and strokes.

Thin line
7.5 mm

Used for smaller details. Spaces between strokes are always 5 mm.

Always scale proportionally for larger icons — the ratios stay the same.

41 — 2D iconography

Iconography is suggestive of the service.

All iconography uses a heavier weight for primary lines. For smaller details — the smile inside the customer-care icon, or corner details on currency icons — drop to the lighter line weight. Aim to maintain similar overall proportions and sizes across the set.

Specific icon details: the customer-care icon uses rounded edges on the smile to better illustrate a smile.

42 — 3D iconography

From 2D to 3D.

In most cases iconography lives in a 3D space. This aligns with the brand's idea of simplicity, achieving a color-on-color effect. Rendering icons in 3D space also adds the idea of a tangible object, creating a look that is simple, approachable, and easy to understand.

43 — Creating 3D iconography

Five Photoshop steps.

The 3D effect is comprised of layer effects in Adobe Photoshop CC. Specific details aren't listed here — a source file is provided to help in the process. When applying the 3D effect at billboard scale, recreate the effect from the source file rather than scaling up.

  1. Background — Create a simple backdrop necessary for the icon to live in.
  2. Icon — Import the chosen icon as a smart object to preserve detail and quality.
  3. Icon effects — Apply Bevel & Emboss, Inner Shadow, and Color Overlay (the Color Overlay matches the background colour).
  4. Shadows — Repeat the icon layer, add a blur, and duplicate multiple times to build a shadow.
  5. Highlight — Sample the lightest colour from the backdrop, apply a gradient, set the layer to Lighter Color blend mode at 40% opacity.

Layer effects note: some layer effects act differently according to the colour. When applying a 'Lighter Color' layer effect on a Gray layout and icon, the opacity may need adjusting.

44 — 3D iconography shadow distance

Shadow distance = 2x stroke.

Depth adds realism. Too shallow looks flat; too deep makes the icon float incorrectly. The right balance is 2x the icon's stroke width — measure the stroke (x), then duplicate it for the shadow offset.

45 — Color environments

Built in Photoshop with a Soft Light gradient.

Environments are only ever used when rendering Qi icons in a 3D effect. In Photoshop apply a Gradient Overlay layer style to any of the primary colours: Blend Mode Soft Light, Opacity 60%, Style Linear, Angle 40°, Scale 100%. Play with the scale to enlarge the darker area.
