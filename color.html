Section 02 — Our Colors

Modern by pairing.

Our colours represent a sense of modernity in their pairing and use. Three primaries — Yellow, Cool Gray, and Bright Teal — plus white. Yellow gives the brand approachability and a fresh outlook; Gray gives it weight and reliability; Teal adds freshness and highlights what matters most. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

28 — Our colors

What each colour does.

Each colour has a job. Used together in the right ratio they build a brand that feels modern, dependable, and warm — never dull, never gimmicky.

Yellow

Approachable.

Yellow adds character to the brand. It's an approachable colour representing a fresh outlook.

Cool Gray

Reliable.

The perfect partner to Yellow — much-needed contrast throughout. Gray suggests a reliable entity.

Bright Teal

Fresh.

Adds a touch of freshness to the entire brand. A useful asset, mainly used to highlight key copy.

White

30 — Color balance

40 / 30 / 20 / 10.

A right balance avoids a dull-looking brand. Use the colours interchangeably to create contrast — never let any one of them dominate the page.

Cool Gray · 40%

The Qi brand is predominantly Gray — but balanced.

Yellow · 30%

Adds brightness; lesser use than Gray. Used interchangeably with Gray to create contrast.

Teal · 20%

Optimal visibility on top of Gray or Yellow. Use as background for specific scenarios or to highlight important copy.

White · 10%

Used sparingly. Typography may also be set in white to add freshness.

Usage

Pairing rules.

Yellow + Gray

The default duo. Use them interchangeably as the foundation of any layout. Gray carries trust; Yellow brings warmth.

Teal as highlight

Reserve Bright Teal for key words, important copy, or accent backgrounds. Don't paint a whole layout in it.

White as oxygen

Use white only when desperately needed — for dense text or busy spreads.

No off-brand colours

Don't introduce new hues. Three primaries plus white cover every case the brand needs.
