72 — Illuminated sign

Acrylic lightbox, internally lit.

A free-standing or wall-mounted illuminated Qi icon sign — used at branch entrances and high-visibility points.

01 · Lightbox

Acrylic lightbox internally illuminated in yellow (closest to Pantone 7405).

02 · Frame

Aluminium frame painted yellow (closest to Pantone 7405).

Color-balance the lighting to maintain consistent yellow — use coloured LEDs or applied gels.

73–75 — Storefront signage

Three options. One is not recommended.

Option 1 · Premium

Halo-lit acrylic letters

  1. Aluminium spray-painted gray fascia (Pantone 5405).
  2. Raised yellow acrylic letters (Pantone 7405) with internally illuminated faces and halo lighting to the rear.
  3. (Suggestion) Illuminated edge in bright teal (Pantone 3262).
Option 2 · Standard

Fret-cut letters

  1. Aluminium spray-painted gray fascia (Pantone 5405).
  2. Yellow fret-cut letters (Pantone 7405).
Option 3 · Economy

Flex signage (canvas)

A cheap, inexpensive way to produce signage. Because flex can't form round shapes, the fascia spans the full building facade.

Note: this route is not recommended.

For all options: color-balance lighting using coloured LEDs or applied gels to maintain consistent yellow.

84–86 — Brochure

Cover 210 × 284.7 mm.

Brochure covers run in the full primary palette — Yellow, Gray, Teal — with bold type covering both Latin and Arabic. Spreads use embossed 3D Qi marks for tactile depth. Cover format: 210 × 284.7 mm.

87 — Posters

Three colour ways. Same system.

Posters carry the 3D iconography centred top, with copy below — Yellow, Gray, and Teal backgrounds rotate to keep the system fresh. Always include the Qi mark bottom-right and the URL bottom-left.

90 — Website

Yellow header bar, gray hero.

The website uses a yellow top bar with the Qi mark left and primary nav right — HOME · About · Products · Services · Contact · Sign in / Sign up. The hero sits on Cool Gray and showcases three 3D service icons in a row: customer support, fast transfers, fast cash loans.

91 — E-mail signature

Logo · Name · Title · Mobile · URL.

Bilingual Qi brandmark at top, then name in Bold Cool Gray, title and mobile in regular Cool Gray, and Qicard.com in Bright Teal at the bottom. Set in Tahoma — the digital-safe fallback.

97 — Environments details

Yellow + Teal furniture.

Branch interiors use a Yellow + Teal palette on furniture — yellow steel frames with teal upholstered cushions. The brand colour pair carries into physical space without losing the system.
