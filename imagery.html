Section 05 — Our Image Style

Used in key moments.

Imagery is reduced to a limited use. The Qi brand is simple, so imagery is reserved for key moments — to add freshness to a page. Two genres only: lifestyle (people in context) and still life (objects on Qi's primary backgrounds).

48 — Lifestyle imagery

Choosing the right image.

Imagery should always represent the Qi ethos. Pick photos that signal one or more of the five qualities below. Avoid stock-y, posed, or overlit shots — Qi's people look real and at ease.

48 — Image categories

Four categories of shot.

Portraits

Portraits are a great way to display the effect Qi has on its customers. Faces telling the brand story.

Lifestyle

Imagery displays a lifestyle that is easy and care-free. This adds value to the Qi brand, inspiring confidence in the company's services.

Services

Display people using services Qi provides. Mobile banking or customer service is represented by a person talking on the phone.

On brand

Imagery makes use of colours that are on brand for added continuity and recognition. Backgrounds and props pull from the primary palette.

49 — Still life

Always on a Primary background.

Still life imagery is always captured against a background using the Primary colours. This adds continuity to the brand and stays true to its simple nature.
