02 — Brand mark

Yellow Q · Arabic wordmark.

The Buy Now Pay Later mark pairs the Qi yellow icon with the Arabic wordmark قسّطها ويه كي ("Pay it in instalments with Qi"). It's a complete unit — icon + wordmark function as one indivisible lockup.

قسّطهاويه كي
03 — Clear space & minimum size

x = the corner Qi mark.

A mandatory empty area surrounds the full lockup. The unit of measure is x — the height of the small Qi mark placed at all four corners of the layout. Maintain at least 1× on the top, bottom, left and right at all times.

  • The mini-Qi mark in the corners is essential — it cannot be removed.
  • The Qi icon and the Arabic wordmark behave as a single visual unit.
  • No text, image, shape, edge or design element may enter the protected clear-space.
  • The dot in the Qi mark = the conceptual separator between logomark and wordmark.
قسّطهاويه كي
Rule

1× clear space minimum

Top, bottom, left and right must each carry at least 1 unit of x — never less. When format allows, give it more. The four corner Qi marks anchor the protected area.

04 — Visual system

النظام البصري.

The visual system unifies every BNPL touchpoint — from social posts and partner lockups to billboards and product cards — under a single Arabic-first language built on yellow, black and white.

05 — Visual usage

Black + yellow on white.

The primary lockup uses black wordmark + yellow Qi icon on a white background. Alternatives are provided so the mark can hold up against any background — always pick the variant that produces the highest contrast for legibility.

On Yellow
Black wordmark

Use the black logo on the yellow brand colour.

On Black
Yellow + white

Yellow Q with white wordmark — for dark photo or solid black.

On White
Primary lockup

Black wordmark + yellow Q. The default everywhere.

Choose the variation that gives the largest contrast against the background. On light photo backgrounds use the black version; on dark photo backgrounds, white.

06 — الاستخدام غير الصحيح

Nine things never to do.

The Qi Installment mark must never be altered or displayed in unapproved ways. The list below covers every common misuse the team must avoid.

01

Never change the brand colour of the mark in any unauthorised way.

02

Never rotate the mark.

03

Never tilt or distort the mark (no skew / perspective).

04

Never reposition the elements of the mark relative to each other.

05

Never change the transparency of the mark.

06

Never create a keyline (outlined) version of the mark.

07

Never violate the Clear Space rules.

08

Never add a shadow to the mark.

09

Never place the mark inside a frame or shape.

07 — Social media · 4:5

Logo and message — diagonal.

When a campaign carries a key promotion, the logo and the message live on opposite diagonals. Logo top-left ↔ message bottom-right, or logo bottom-right ↔ message top-left. Logo and message must always be adjacent across the diagonal — never side-by-side.

  • Keep the message inside the outlined Box on the bottom-right of the layout.
  • The four corner Qi marks must always be present.

Rule

Logo opposite the message

When the message is in the bottom-right, the logo sits in the top-left, and vice-versa. Top-right messages flip the logo to the bottom-left.

The headline always stays inside the outlined "Box" on the bottom side of the format.

08 — Social media · 1:1

Square posts — same diagonal rule.

For 1:1 posts (short messages, user reviews, merchant highlights) the logo lives in the bottom-left corner and the message in the top-right — or flipped, with the logo top-left and the message bottom-right.

10 — Partner co-branding

Qi Installment first. 80px gap.

When co-branded with partners — Klarna, VISA, SONY, etc. — the Qi Installment lockup always appears first, followed by the partner brand. The distance between the two marks is fixed at X = 80 px (twice the dot in the Qi mark).

  • Treat each brand mark with care; both must look balanced and professional together.
  • Use X (the dot in the Qi mark, doubled) as the spacing unit between Qi and the partner mark.
  • X = 80 px.
  • This rule preserves identity, ensures correct visual sequencing, and creates a consistent visual relationship across every shared asset.

Non-digital channels.

For physical / printed touchpoints — roll-up banners, A4/A3 posters, billboards, lightboxes — the same visual rules apply at scale. Every format carries the four corner Qi marks, the lockup in one corner and the message in the opposite.
