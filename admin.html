I — Rule relations

Rules talk to each other.

Define multipliers that fire when one category fails. e.g. "Color FAIL → penalise everything by ×1.2" or "Logo weak → make platform safety stricter".

J — Learning insights

The brain learns from you.

Every audit and every override is logged. When auto-adjust is on, the brain quietly retunes tolerances and weights based on what reviewers consistently override.

K — Brand DNA

Train the brain on great work.

Add reference designs from the agent (each "Run audit" lets you press + Add as reference). Their fingerprint averages into your Brand DNA — the brain then catches designs that pass every rule but don't look like Qi.
