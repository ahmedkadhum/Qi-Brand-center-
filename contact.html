Trademarks

Use of the Qi name & mark.

Qi, the Qi icon, the Qi wordmark, and the Qi colors are trademarks of Qi. Use them only as set out in these guidelines, and only with our written consent for any commercial purpose.

✓ Do
  • Refer to Qi as a noun ("a Qi account").
  • Use the approved logo files at the correct minimum size.
  • Apply the approved color values from the Color page.
✕ Don't
  • Don't use the Qi name as a verb or in product names without consent.
  • Don't recreate the logo or alter its proportions.
  • Don't imply endorsement of products or services not made by Qi.
